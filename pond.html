The small rules of the pond

How does life continue?

Each organism searches for food. Movement and sensing spend energy. Enough stored energy produces an offspring; depleted energy or old age ends a life.

Speed
Faster swimming costs more energy.
Sense
Food is detected from farther away, at an energy cost.
Thrift
Reduces energy cost, slows movement and raises the reproduction threshold.
Inheritance
Offspring inherit traits with small variations. The lineage color stays the same.

This is a toy model for exploring the consequences of rules, not a biological simulator or a real-world prediction.
