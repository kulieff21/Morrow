The original question

Does a leader in abundance keep its lead in drought?

Instead of comparing two unrelated worlds, we cloned the complete state at day 8. Organisms, genes, ages, energy, existing food and the random generator state began identically.

One branch kept full food renewal; the other received 15%. Both continued to day 16. Leadership meant living descendant count, with ties preserved.

From observation to intervention

What if we change Çiy's thrift gene?

We committed the follow-up protocol before examining its four-arm results. We reused the same twelve starting conditions and set living Çiy organisms' thrift to 1.00 once at day 8. Descendants continued to mutate normally.

The mean intervention-by-environment interaction was −12.40 percentage points in share and +6.50 organisms in count. Thrift couples energy use, movement, reproduction thresholds and lifespan.

What about a hundred new worlds?

This is replication across new initial conditions in one fixed model. The consecutive seed range is not a random sample; the bootstrap interval describes resampling of the selected ensemble. Coverage across all seeds and biological validity are not established. All nineteen reversed outcomes remain in the evidence. Thrift still changes several coupled mechanisms.

Inside the small world

Explicit rules behind the apparent life.

↝

Speed

Moves farther. Energy expenditure grows with speed squared.

◎

Sense

Finds food farther away. Sensing also has an energy cost.

◒

Thrift

Lowers energy cost, slows movement, raises the reproduction threshold and age limit.

The organisms do not use AI. There is no learning, predation, mating or real genetics. Lineage names are retained from the original Turkish model: Akım, Çiy, Köz, Sis, Yankı and Filiz.

Fixed steps, seeded randomness and full checkpoints make outcomes reproducible. Replay requires the same rules and interventions; a seed alone is not enough.
